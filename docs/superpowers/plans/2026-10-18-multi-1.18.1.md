**Goal:** (1) Nothing in claude-discord is keyed by a bot's NAME alone any more: a manager's backend records, its scratch, the keeper revival and the "a manager of that name exists" refusals all key on `<key>/<name>`, so two projects may each have a manager called `mgr`. (2) The 1.8-1.16 compat paths go: `~/.claude-discord/hooks`, `~/.claude-discord/compat*`, `sync_compat`/`compat_if_stale`/`same_tree`, `patch --compat`'s rebuild; `records/projects` retires in favour of `projects/*/project`.

**Tech Stack:** as 1.18.0 (bash 3.2 syntax, jq 1.6, BSD userland in the wrapper; the suite is Linux-only). Python 3 + Textual for the TUI (`load_names`, `--state-dir`).

Base: `main` at `cac974f` (1.18.0). Branch: `feat/multi-1.18.1`.

**Scope:** Tasks 4 and 5b of the 1.17.0 plan as carried into `2026-10-18-move-1.18.0.md` (decided 2026-10-16 with junyong-dev-bot and Junyong), plus the three items 1.18.0 left for them on purpose (its "Changed while implementing (Task 3)"): `records/monitor/<bot>` -> `records/monitor/<key>/<bot>`, `scratch/<bot>` -> `scratch/<key>/<bot>`, `records/projects` -> `projects/*/project`.
